# Not built — and why

Every idea that was dropped, postponed, or checked before being adopted. Newest at the
top. **Read this before proposing a new tool**: the idea may already have a verdict.

Each entry answers four questions:

- **Verdict** — dropped / postponed / adopted.
- **Evidence** — what decided it: a count, a measurement, a reading. Not a feeling.
- **Reason** — one or two sentences. For a drop, say whether the justification is
  *missing* (closed for good) or *not yet there* (postponed).
- **Reopen if** — the precise event that would justify looking again. "Never" is allowed.
  "Maybe later" isn't: it's how ideas come back brand new.

The entries below are examples, in the shape of the ones from the episode. Replace them
with yours.

---

## Email-sorting agent

- **Verdict:** dropped — no justification.
- **Evidence:** a count of email headers by month over ten months, thirteen calls, no
  email opened: four or five conversations a week.
- **Reason:** that reads in thirty seconds. An agent for it is vanity engineering. What
  was really missing was a *reminder* to answer, not a *summary*.
- **Reopen if:** the weekly volume is multiplied by ten and stays there for a month.

## Local AI model, to save on API calls

- **Verdict:** dropped — no justification.
- **Evidence:** measured cost per run of the daily job: about eleven cents.
- **Reason:** nothing to save. The idea came from an architect's intuition; the
  measurement closed it.
- **Reopen if:** a need that a local model answers for another reason than cost
  (offline work, privacy of a specific document).

## Daily ritual with a keyword

- **Verdict:** dropped.
- **Evidence:** the ritual was designed for two projects; there are now seven.
- **Reason:** it made the human carry the burden of remembering to do it. Replaced by a
  question the AI answers on demand ("where am I on X?").
- **Reopen if:** never in this form.

## A "humor" skill

- **Verdict:** dropped — wrong mechanism.
- **Evidence:** no situation could be named in which the skill would fire.
- **Reason:** a skill without a natural trigger never gets used. Became one line in the
  kernel instead.
- **Reopen if:** never as a skill.
