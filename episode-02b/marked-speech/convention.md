## Who's speaking — the marking convention

In every file of this workspace: **`{S: …}` marks Sam's voice** — a decision, a ruling, a
wording that is his. Everything else is synthesis and logging produced by an AI, and does
not carry the same weight.

Three consequences:

- What is inside `{S: …}` is never rephrased or compressed. The rest may be.
  **One exception: dictated speech.** Hesitations, repeated words, restarted sentences
  can be removed to keep the meaning. The meaning itself is not touched.
- A claim that comes from an outside source (a law, a study, a contract, a website)
  carries its source in the text. Without a named source, it is assumed to be the AI's.
- The convention does not apply retroactively. Older content is assumed unattributed and
  is not reconstructed: nobody knows any more who said what.

Why: an agent's conclusion written into a note comes back in the next session with the
authority of a fact, and gets passed on without being questioned.
